    About Us

    A Taste of Solapur's Finest

    Ajinkya AC Veg Restaurant and Lodging is one of Solapur's most beloved vegetarian dining destinations. Nestled at Lucky Chowk in the heart of Murarji Peth, we have been serving authentic Maharashtrian and Indian vegetarian cuisine to locals and travelers alike.

    Whether you're craving our famous Solapuri Misal, a comforting Thali, or need a clean, comfortable room for your stay — Ajinkya is your home away from home. We pride ourselves on affordable pricing, warm hospitality, and flavors that keep you coming back.
